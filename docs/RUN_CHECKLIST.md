# Morning run checklist

Night before
- Open the app once on Wi-Fi. Footer should read "Gemma ready, on device".
- Accept Location, Motion, Microphone and Health prompts if any are still pending.
- Settings: set Daily target to 20 min so a 25-30 min run reaches every layer.
- Settings: Test mode OFF. "Match tempo to my stride" ON.
- Charge AirPods.

At the door
1. AirPods in, open Grow a Song.
2. Tap "Start run". The drone starts right away.
3. Wait for the footer to say "Composed in ...s" (about 5-15 s) before locking the phone.
   Gemma needs the screen on; the app keeps it awake while composing.
4. Optional: tap "Capture sound" and hold the phone up for 10 s. The sound of the place
   becomes the last layer. Without it, a built-in wind-and-birds texture plays.
5. Lock the phone, pocket it, run.

What you should hear
- A new layer fades in at 0, 2, 5, 9, 14 and 20 counted minutes (20 min target).
- Tempo drifts toward your stride within about 20 s of reaching a steady cadence.
- Minutes only count after civil dawn (sun above -6 deg) and while moving on foot.

After
- Tap End. Then wait a few seconds with the screen on for Gemma's field note.
- Week tab: play or export today's song (share icon).

If something goes wrong
- No layers growing: check the "Counting / Not counting" line. Before dawn nothing counts.
  Test mode counts every minute regardless.
- AirPods disconnect pauses the song on purpose (no pocket speaker blast). Tap play.
